How Insurance Works on CareConnect AI

1

Enter Insurance Details During Booking

2

Doctor Verifies at Clinic

The clinic verifies your insurance coverage at the time of your visit.

3

Cashless or Reimbursement

Depending on your plan, the consultation may be cashless or eligible for reimbursement from your insurer.

Disclaimer: Insurance coverage varies by plan and doctor. CareConnect AI does not process insurance claims directly. Always confirm coverage with your insurer and the clinic before your appointment.
