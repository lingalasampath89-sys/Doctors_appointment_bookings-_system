Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Effective immediately

1 Information We Collect

When you use CareConnect AI, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, email address provided during booking.
  • Health Information: Symptoms and appointment notes you voluntarily share.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited and search terms to improve our service.
  • Device Data: Browser type and IP address for security purposes.

2 How We Use Your Data

  • Facilitate appointment bookings and send confirmations/reminders.
  • Allow doctors to view relevant appointment details only.
  • Improve platform performance and user experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements in India.

3 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with the doctor you book with (limited to appointment details) and trusted infrastructure partners under strict confidentiality agreements.

4 Data Security

We implement AES-256 encryption for stored data, HTTPS for all transmissions, regular security audits, and strict internal access controls to protect your information.

5 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Email us at privacy@careconnect.ai to exercise your rights.

6 Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@careconnect.ai or write to CareConnect AI, HITEC City, Hyderabad, Telangana — 500081.
