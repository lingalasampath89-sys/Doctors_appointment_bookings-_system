Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is booking an appointment free?
Yes! Creating an account and booking appointments on CareConnect AI is completely free for patients. You only pay the doctor's consultation fee.
Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
Yes, you can cancel or reschedule up to 2 hours before your appointment slot from your dashboard. Cancellation policies may vary by doctor.
Are the doctors on CareConnect AI verified?
Absolutely. All doctors on our platform are verified through MCI/NMC registration checks and hold valid medical licences. We also monitor patient reviews continuously.
Will I receive a confirmation after booking?
Yes! You will receive an instant confirmation via email with all appointment details, including the doctor's name, date, time, and location.
